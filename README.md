# Gus

Gus is a little man in a Hawaiian shirt who lives on your desktop. He strolls
along the bottom of your screen, sometimes hops, and sometimes drops in from
the top on a rope or floats down under a parachute.

Click him and he turns round, drops his trousers and farts right at you: a
green flash, gas clouds swelling toward the screen, a shockwave, stink lines
and a big comic **PFFFRRT!**

## The game

- **Save him with a click.** Click Gus once while he's on screen and he's saved.
  He farts, pulls his trousers up and leaves.
- **He comes back faster.** Every save in a row makes his next visit 10% faster,
  with speed lines streaking behind him.
- **Don't leave him hanging.** If nobody clicks him in time (5 seconds by
  default), he swells up, turns green, shakes, and explodes into a huge fart
  cloud. That ends your streak, and he's back to normal speed.
- **His shirt keeps score.** The number on his belly, and in big letters on his
  back, is how many times he's ever been clicked.

He shows up in different ways each visit: walking in from either side, lowered
on a rope, or parachuting in, each at a random spot.

## The app

Open **Gus** from the app launcher to control him:

- Start and stop him, or summon him right now
- How often he shows up, how long he wanders, and how fast he walks
- Whether he explodes if you don't click him, and after how many seconds
- Sound on or off, and the volume
- Which of his seven farts he does, with a Play button to hear each one

He keeps going after you close the window.

| Key   | Does                       |
|-------|----------------------------|
| Space | Start or stop Gus          |
| S     | Summon Gus now             |
| M     | Sound on or off            |
| P     | Play a fart                |
| ?     | Show or hide shortcuts     |
| Q     | Quit (Gus keeps running)   |

## The farts

Gus has seven, all generated by `overlay/make_assets.py` at build time, so the
project ships no audio files: The Tuba, The Squeaker, The Machine Gun, The Wet
Flap, The Question Mark, The Opera and Silent But Deadly. Each one is built like
a real fart: pressure pulses that wobble in pitch, wet bubbles, a sputtering
tail and a little room echo. There's an eighth for when he explodes.

## Install

Gus is made for Linux desktops running Wayland with
[Quickshell](https://quickshell.org) (Omarchy and Hyprland setups have it). You
also need Qt 6, PipeWire (`pw-play`) and Python 3 to build.

```sh
git clone <this repo>
cd gus
bin/install-user    # installs into ~/.local, no password needed
```

Then press Super + Space and open **Gus**. On Arch-based systems,
`bin/install` builds and installs a pacman package instead.

## How it's built

- `overlay/` is Gus himself: a Quickshell overlay (`shell.qml`) that covers the
  screen but only catches clicks on Gus, and `Gus.qml`, his rig of shaded
  vector parts so every limb can move.
- `src/` is the control app: C++ and Qt Quick.
- Settings live in `~/.config/gus/settings.json`; his click count and streak in
  `~/.local/state/gus/stats.json`.

```sh
bin/build     # build/gus, with the overlay in build/overlay
bin/test      # tests, run offscreen
```
